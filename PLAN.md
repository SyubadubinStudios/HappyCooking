# Happy Cooking: Roadmap / Rencana Pengembangan

*Dibuat oleh Ariana Mischa Fadhila dari Syubadubin Studios.* Source of truth for features: [solution-design.md](solution-design.md). Progress log: [Progress.md](Progress.md).

## English

### v1.0: First playable release ✅ (current)

- 6 islands, 33 recipes (25 regional, 8 creative), 6 step mini-games, 1–3 star results with fun facts
- Restaurant with guests, tips, a rating and 8 decorations
- Farm (12 crops, real-time growth, rain bonus), fishing (10 fish, night-only species)
- 8 pets with bonuses, 18 story quests, 14 trophies, 5 seasonal events
- Offline friends and gift codes, a day/night cycle and weather
- SQLite autosave, synced-folder cloud save
- English and Bahasa Indonesia throughout
- Desktop (Windows installer), with Android and iOS launchers

### v1.1: Content and polish

- [ ] More recipes per island (target 60+), more ingredients and crops
- [ ] Character voice lines (EN/ID) and a richer music set
- [ ] Accessibility: larger text option, colour-blind safe zones, haptics on mobile, honouring the reduced-motion setting in 3D
- [ ] Touch-first layout pass for phones (portrait-safe panels)
- [ ] Parent corner: play-time summary and the dishes the child learned

### v1.2: Mobile release

- [ ] Signed Android release (Play Store) and iOS build on macOS (App Store, kids category compliance)
- [ ] Platform cloud save (Google Play Games, iCloud key-value) in addition to synced folders
- [ ] Performance profiling on low-end tablets

### v2.0: Social and online

- [ ] Real-time online multiplayer: cook together and visit friends' restaurants. Today, social features are offline friend and gift codes only.
- [ ] A cloud backend with child-safe accounts managed by parents. Today, cloud save is a synced folder.
- [ ] Shared seasonal community goals

### v2.x: Future expansions (from the SDD)

- [ ] Ocean Cuisine · [ ] Space Kitchen · [ ] Dino Chef World · [ ] Mythological Cuisine · [ ] Fantasy Dessert Kingdom
- [ ] 300+ recipes in total (currently 33)
- [ ] 3D pet models that follow the player (pets are 2D portraits today)
- [ ] Restaurant building: free placement and an expandable floor plan (decorations use fixed spots today)
- [ ] More 3D models for regional dishes (7 dishes are sculpted; the rest use procedural plates)

## Bahasa Indonesia

### v1.0: Rilis pertama yang bisa dimainkan ✅ (sekarang)

- 6 pulau, 33 resep (25 resep daerah, 8 kreasi), 6 jenis mini-game, hasil 1–3 bintang beserta fakta menarik
- Restoran dengan tamu, tip, rating, dan 8 dekorasi
- Kebun (12 tanaman, tumbuh real-time, bonus hujan), memancing (10 ikan, ada yang hanya muncul malam)
- 8 hewan peliharaan dengan bonus, 18 misi cerita, 14 trofi, 5 event musiman
- Kode teman dan hadiah tanpa internet, siklus siang/malam dan cuaca
- Autosave SQLite, simpan di cloud lewat folder tersinkron
- Bahasa Indonesia dan Inggris di seluruh game
- Desktop (installer Windows), dengan peluncur Android dan iOS

### v1.1: Konten dan pemolesan

- [ ] Lebih banyak resep per pulau (target 60+), lebih banyak bahan dan tanaman
- [ ] Suara tokoh (EN/ID) dan pilihan musik yang lebih kaya
- [ ] Aksesibilitas: opsi teks besar, zona aman buta warna, getaran di ponsel, menghormati pengaturan kurangi-gerak di 3D
- [ ] Penyesuaian tata letak untuk ponsel (panel aman untuk layar tegak)
- [ ] Pojok orang tua: ringkasan waktu bermain dan hidangan yang dipelajari anak

### v1.2: Rilis mobile

- [ ] Rilis Android bertanda tangan (Play Store) dan build iOS di macOS (App Store, kepatuhan kategori anak)
- [ ] Simpan cloud bawaan platform (Google Play Games, iCloud) selain folder tersinkron
- [ ] Profiling performa di tablet kelas bawah

### v2.0: Sosial dan online

- [ ] Multiplayer online real-time: masak bersama dan kunjungi restoran teman. Saat ini fitur sosial hanya berupa kode teman dan hadiah tanpa internet.
- [ ] Backend cloud dengan akun ramah anak yang dikelola orang tua. Saat ini simpan cloud memakai folder tersinkron.
- [ ] Target bersama komunitas saat event musiman

### v2.x: Ekspansi mendatang (dari SDD)

- [ ] Ocean Cuisine · [ ] Space Kitchen · [ ] Dino Chef World · [ ] Mythological Cuisine · [ ] Fantasy Dessert Kingdom
- [ ] Total 300+ resep (saat ini 33)
- [ ] Model 3D hewan peliharaan yang mengikuti pemain (saat ini berupa potret 2D)
- [ ] Membangun restoran: penempatan bebas dan denah yang bisa diperluas (saat ini dekorasi memakai posisi tetap)
- [ ] Lebih banyak model 3D hidangan daerah (7 hidangan sudah dipahat; sisanya memakai piring prosedural)
